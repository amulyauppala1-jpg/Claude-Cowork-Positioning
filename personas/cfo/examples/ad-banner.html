Why this CTA

A CFO does not want to start anything. They want a number — so the CTA promises the number the rest of the campaign is honest about not having yet.

Headlines considered

“Your fourth AI subscription is the problem.” Lands the duplicate-spend point harder, but opens on an accusation — and this buyer approved those three.

“Every knowledge worker. One vendor.” Cleanest statement of vp-coverage, but reads as a capability claim rather than a spend claim. Works on the page, not in an ad.

Draft. Grounded in products/cowork-enterprise.md and personas/cfo/README.md (updated 2026-09-05, status: draft). Leads with vp-coverage per the CFO's ranking for an enterprise motion. Steered around the persona's Avoid list — productivity, efficiency, transformation, headcount. No pricing content exists in the repo, so the CTA drives to the analysis that produces the number rather than asserting one.
