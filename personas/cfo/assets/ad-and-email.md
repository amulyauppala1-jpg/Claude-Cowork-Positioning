## Ad unit — 728×90 and 300×250

**Recommended**

> **Headline:** One deployment, not another line item.
> **Sub-headline:** Cowork consolidates the AI assistants you already pay for — deployed once, configured per function.
> **CTA:** See the math

### Alternates considered

| Headline | Why not |
|---|---|
| *Your fourth AI subscription is the problem.* | Sharper, and it lands the duplicate-spend point harder — but it opens on an accusation, and a CFO who has approved those three reads it as blame. |
| *Every knowledge worker. One vendor.* | Cleanest statement of `vp-coverage`, but it reads as a capability claim rather than a spend claim, so it works better on the page than in an ad. |

**Why "See the math" and not "Get started."** A CFO does not want to start
anything. They want a number, and the CTA should promise the number the rest of
the campaign is honest about not having yet.

## Email

**Subject:** Three AI line items, one renewal
**Preview:** What consolidating them would actually cost

> Most enterprises we talk to are now paying for AI in at least three places —
> a chat assistant, a copilot, and something a function bought on its own. At
> median scale that's around 15% of SaaS spend sitting on duplicates, and each
> duplicate now carries an AI surcharge on top of the base fee.
>
> Cowork is deployed once by IT and configured per function, so it consolidates
> those rather than joining them. More than 90% of its usage isn't software
> development — business operations and content creation are the largest
> categories — which is the part that makes one deployment cover everyone
> instead of becoming an engineering tool with a company-wide licence.
>
> Worth 20 minutes to map what your teams pay for today against what this would
> replace. That produces the per-seat comparison this decision turns on.
>
> — [Name]

*128 words. One ask.*

## Vocabulary check

Steered around, from the CFO's Avoid list:

| Avoided | Why |
|---|---|
| **productivity** | Burned. Every AI vendor has used it and the ROI data hasn't substantiated it. |
| **efficiency** (unquantified) | Reads as a dodge to this buyer. |
| **transformation** | Reads as expensive and slow. |
| **headcount / FTE savings** | A CFO will hold you to it at renewal. Promise capacity, not reductions. |

Anchored to **avoided spend** rather than projected gains — more credible to this
audience, per the persona file.
