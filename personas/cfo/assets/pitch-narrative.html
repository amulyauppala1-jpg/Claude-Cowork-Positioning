One deployment, not another line item.

Cowork is an agentic workspace deployed once by IT and configured per function — so it consolidates the assistants you're already paying for rather than joining them.

  • One vendor across every function. One relationship, one renewal, one security review.
  • Cloud-run for restricted estates. Windows-locked and VDI machines that could never take a desktop install.
  • Configured per function. Plugins for finance, legal, sales and marketing, connected to the tools those teams already use.
